Southern Banana Pudding Ice Cream

2-quart Cuisinart formulation · Target finished yield: approximately 1¾–2 quarts including mix-ins, depending on expansion and portion used. Chill 8 hours; harden 4–6 hours.

Real ripe bananas in a vanilla custard, concentrated banana-pudding ribbon, and buttery Nilla Wafer crumble. Designed for strong banana flavor, controlled sweetness, and thick milkshakes.

Ingredients

Banana custard base

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 3/4 cups whole milk
  • 3 medium very ripe bananas (about 1–1 1/4 cups mashed)
  • 5 large egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup light corn syrup
  • 1/4 cup nonfat dry milk powder
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp banana extract (optional, only if needed)

Concentrated banana ribbon

  • 2 medium very ripe bananas
  • 2 tbsp light brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp whole milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of kosher salt

Buttery Nilla Wafer crumble

  • 30 Nilla Wafers
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • Pinch of kosher salt

Directions

1. Prepare the banana custard

  1. Mash 3 very ripe bananas until smooth.
  2. Combine heavy cream, whole milk, mashed bananas and dry milk powder in a saucepan. Heat over medium-low until steaming, stirring frequently. Do not boil.
  3. Remove from heat, cover and steep 15–20 minutes, then blend briefly until smooth.
  4. Whisk 5 egg yolks with 1/2 cup sugar in a separate bowl. Slowly whisk in about 1 cup warm banana mixture, then return everything to the saucepan.
  5. Add corn syrup and cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, to 170–175°F.
  6. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla and salt. Cool rapidly in an ice bath and refrigerate at least 8 hours, preferably overnight.
  7. Taste the cold custard. If banana flavor is weak, add banana extract 1/8 tsp at a time, up to 1/4 tsp.

2. Make the concentrated banana ribbon

  1. Mash 2 ripe bananas.
  2. Melt 2 tbsp butter over medium-low heat. Add bananas, brown sugar and salt.
  3. Cook 7–10 minutes, stirring frequently, until thick and slightly caramelized.
  4. Stir in 2 tbsp whole milk and 1 tsp vanilla, cook another minute, then chill completely. The ribbon should be thick and spoonable.

3. Make the buttery Nilla Wafer crumble

  1. Heat oven to 325°F.
  2. Crush 30 Nilla Wafers into coarse pieces and toss with 3 tbsp melted butter and a pinch of salt.
  3. Bake on parchment 7–9 minutes, stirring once. Cool completely.
  4. Reserve about one-quarter of the crumble for toppings.

4. Churn in the 2-quart Cuisinart

  1. Freeze the Cuisinart bowl completely. Check the manufacturer's maximum liquid fill level; churn in two batches if needed.
  2. Stir or briefly blend the cold custard.
  3. Start the machine before pouring in the base. Churn to soft-serve consistency, typically 20–30 minutes.
  4. In the final minute, add about half of the crumble not reserved for toppings.
  5. Transfer immediately to a freezer-safe container.

5. Layer and harden

  1. Spread one-third of the ice cream into a freezer container.
  2. Dot with banana ribbon and sprinkle with some remaining wafer crumble.
  3. Repeat twice. Run a butter knife through once or twice without blending the ribbon fully.
  4. Cover tightly and freeze 4–6 hours.

Tips & upgrades

Flavor: Use heavily spotted ripe bananas. Taste the fully chilled base before churning; if needed, add banana extract only 1/8 tsp at a time.
Texture: Real banana purée may make the ice cream firmer than plain vanilla. Corn syrup helps; don't add extra purée without rebalancing. Keep banana ribbon in thin streaks because fruit-based ribbons may freeze firm.
Capacity: Two quarts is the machine's capacity, not necessarily its maximum liquid-base fill. Check your model's instructions; never exceed its fill limit. If necessary, churn in batches.

Southern Banana Pudding Milkshake

For one large milkshake: blend 3 generous scoops banana pudding ice cream with 1/4 cup whole milk and 1 tbsp crushed Nilla Wafers. Add 1/4 ripe banana and 1/4 tsp vanilla if desired. Top with whipped cream and wafer crumbs.
