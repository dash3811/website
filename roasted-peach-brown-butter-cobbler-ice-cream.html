Roasted Peach & Brown Butter Cobbler Ice Cream

Roasted peach ice cream with brown butter, buttery cobbler crumble, and a concentrated peach ribbon.

Yield: About 1 quart

Ingredients

Roasted Peaches

  • 4 medium ripe peaches, about 1 1/2 lb
  • 1 tbsp light brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch kosher salt

Brown Butter Peach Base

  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp light corn syrup
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • Roasted peach puree, about 1 to 1 1/4 cups
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste or extract
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/8 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp bourbon (optional)

Brown Butter Cobbler Crumble

  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup old-fashioned oats
  • 2 tbsp light brown sugar
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tbsp heavy cream
  • Tiny pinch flaky or kosher salt (optional)

Concentrated Peach Ribbon

  • 1/2 cup reserved roasted peaches
  • 1 tbsp light brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp water, only if needed

Directions

1. Roast the Peaches

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Peel the peaches if you want a completely smooth base, then cut them into roughly 1/2-inch pieces.
  3. Toss with brown sugar, melted butter, lemon juice, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt.
  4. Spread on a parchment-lined baking sheet and roast 30-40 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the peaches are soft with caramelized edges and very little watery juice remains.
  5. Cool completely. Reserve 1/2 cup for the ribbon and puree the rest until smooth. Aim for about 1 to 1 1/4 cups puree.

2. Make the Brown Butter Peach Base

  1. Brown 4 tablespoons butter over medium heat until the milk solids are deep golden and nutty-smelling.
  2. Add heavy cream, whole milk, milk powder, and corn syrup. Whisk thoroughly and heat until steaming but not boiling.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk egg yolks with granulated sugar.
  4. Slowly whisk about 1 cup of the hot dairy into the yolks to temper them.
  5. Return everything to the saucepan and cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until it reaches 170-175°F.
  6. Remove from heat. Add peach puree, vanilla, salt, cinnamon, and optional bourbon.
  7. Blend until completely smooth. Taste to make sure the peach flavor is unmistakable.
  8. Cover and refrigerate at least 8 hours or overnight.

3. Make the Brown Butter Cobbler Crumble

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Brown the butter until golden brown and nutty-smelling.
  3. Combine flour, oats, brown sugar, cinnamon, baking powder, and salt.
  4. Add brown butter, vanilla, and heavy cream. Mix gently until irregular clusters form.
  5. Spread on parchment and bake 12-16 minutes, stirring once halfway through.
  6. Cool completely. Optionally finish with a tiny pinch of salt.

4. Make the Concentrated Peach Ribbon

  1. Combine reserved roasted peaches, brown sugar, butter, lemon juice, vanilla, and salt in a small saucepan.
  2. Cook over medium-low heat for 8-12 minutes, crushing the peaches as they cook.
  3. Continue until the mixture is thick like peach preserves rather than peach sauce.
  4. If it gets too dry before the peaches break down, add up to 1 tablespoon water.
  5. Cool completely before using.

5. Churn the Ice Cream

  1. Start the Cuisinart before pouring in the base. The paddle should already be moving when the chilled liquid touches the frozen bowl.
  2. Pour the base in steadily through the opening.
  3. Churn only until soft-serve consistency. Do not wait for it to become hard ice cream in the machine.
  4. During the final minute, add about 1/2 to 3/4 cup cobbler crumble.
  5. Transfer the ice cream out of the frozen bowl promptly when churning is finished.

6. Add the Peach Ribbon

  1. Transfer about 1 inch of ice cream into a freezer-safe container.
  2. Spoon small streaks of concentrated peach ribbon over it.
  3. Continue layering ice cream and ribbon, using about 1/4 cup ribbon total.
  4. Make no more than one or two gentle passes with a butter knife if desired. Do not stir.
  5. Freeze 4-6 hours or until firm.

Tips & Upgrades

Choose Peaches by Smell: Ripe, fragrant peaches matter more than perfect appearance. If they are hard and nearly odorless, let them ripen on the counter first.
Concentrate the Fruit: Roasting removes water and intensifies peach flavor. The separate ribbon gets reduced even further, so there is no need for freeze-dried peach powder.
If the Peaches Are Weak: Use 5 peaches instead of 4, roast them all, and still target about 1 to 1 1/4 cups concentrated puree for the base.
Don't Overdo Cinnamon: Peach should be the first flavor you taste. Cinnamon is only there to support the cobbler profile.
Scoopability: The 1 1/2-cup cream / 1 1/2-cup milk balance and 2 tablespoons corn syrup are intentional to help the finished ice cream stay softer in a normal home freezer.
Bourbon Upgrade: Add 1 tablespoon bourbon to the finished custard before chilling for extra depth and a slight boost in scoopability.
Cuisinart Bowl Tip: Start the machine first, pour the chilled base while the paddle is moving, and transfer the ice cream out as soon as it reaches soft-serve consistency.

Roasted Peach Cobbler Milkshake

Blend 3 generous scoops Roasted Peach & Brown Butter Cobbler Ice Cream with 1/4 cup whole milk, 1 tablespoon cobbler crumble, and 1 teaspoon concentrated peach ribbon until smooth and thick.

For more cobbler flavor throughout the shake, pulse 1 tablespoon crumble into fine crumbs first, then add the ice cream and milk. Top with whipped cream, extra crumble, and a little peach ribbon.
