Sausage, Egg & Cheese Waffle Sandwich

A crispy, savory breakfast sandwich with melted cheese and just enough maple butter to complement the sausage without making it overly sweet.

Yield: 2 sandwiches

Ingredients

  • 4 frozen waffles
  • 1/2 lb ground breakfast sausage
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 slices American or cheddar cheese
  • 1 tbsp butter, plus a little more for cooking the eggs
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 2 crispy hash brown patties or portions of homemade hash browns (optional)
  • Hot sauce (optional)

Directions

1. Cook the Waffles

  1. Heat a skillet over medium-low heat.
  2. Place the frozen waffles directly in the skillet.
  3. Cook for 2-3 minutes per side, until hot and crispy. Set aside.

2. Make the Sausage Patties

  1. Divide the breakfast sausage into two equal portions.
  2. Form thin patties slightly larger than the waffles because they will shrink while cooking.
  3. Cook over medium heat for 3-4 minutes per side, until browned and fully cooked.
  4. Put one cheese slice on each patty during the final minute. Cover the skillet briefly to melt the cheese.

3. Cook the Eggs

  1. Lightly beat the eggs with salt and black pepper.
  2. Melt a small amount of butter in the skillet over medium-low heat.
  3. Pour in the eggs and let them begin setting.
  4. Fold the edges inward to form two squares about the size of the waffles.
  5. Flip and cook until just set.

4. Make the Maple Butter

  1. Mix 1 tablespoon softened butter with 1 tablespoon maple syrup and a tiny pinch of salt.
  2. Use a thin layer so the sandwich is lightly sweet without becoming soggy or slippery.

5. Assemble and Toast

  1. Spread a thin layer of maple butter on the inside of each waffle.
  2. Layer each sandwich in this order: waffle, egg, sausage with melted cheese, optional crispy hash browns, optional hot sauce, and the second waffle.
  3. Return the assembled sandwiches to the skillet over medium-low heat.
  4. Cook for about 1 minute per side, pressing gently, until everything is hot and the waffles are extra crisp.

Tips

Keep the sweetness balanced: Spread the maple butter very thinly. You can always serve extra maple syrup on the side.
For homemade hash browns: Squeeze the shredded potatoes extremely dry before frying, and add them to the sandwich only after they are deeply browned and crisp.
Shape the patties wide and thin: Breakfast sausage shrinks as it cooks, so start with patties slightly larger than the waffles.
