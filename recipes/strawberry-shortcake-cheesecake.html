Strawberry Shortcake Cheesecake Ice Cream

Yield: About 1 quart

Ingredients

Roasted Strawberries

  • 1 lb fresh strawberries, hulled and quartered
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Strawberry Cheesecake Ice Cream Base

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste or extract
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 2/3 of roasted strawberries, pureed
  • 2 tbsp freeze-dried strawberry powder (optional)

Strawberry Ribbon

  • 1/3 of roasted strawberries
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
  • Pinch of salt

Buttery Shortcake Crumble

  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 4 tbsp cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp melted butter (optional)
  • Pinch flaky sea salt (optional)

Directions

1. Roast the Strawberries

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Hull and quarter the strawberries. Toss with sugar, lemon juice, vanilla, and salt.
  3. Spread in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  4. Roast 25–35 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until the strawberries are very soft and the juices are thick and syrupy.
  5. Cool. Reserve about 1/3 for the strawberry ribbon and puree the remaining 2/3 until smooth.

2. Make the Strawberry Cheesecake Base

  1. Combine heavy cream, whole milk, and nonfat dry milk powder in a saucepan. Whisk thoroughly to dissolve the milk powder.
  2. Heat over medium heat until steaming but not boiling.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the egg yolks and 1/3 cup sugar.
  4. Slowly whisk about 1 cup of the hot dairy into the egg yolks to temper them.
  5. Pour everything back into the saucepan and cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until the custard reaches 170–175°F.
  6. Remove from heat. Add cream cheese, vanilla, kosher salt, strawberry puree, and optional freeze-dried strawberry powder.
  7. Blend with an immersion blender until completely smooth.
  8. Cover and refrigerate for at least 8 hours or overnight.

3. Make the Strawberry Ribbon

  1. Combine the reserved roasted strawberries, 1 tablespoon sugar, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt in a small saucepan.
  2. Cook over medium-low heat for 5–8 minutes, crushing the strawberries as they cook.
  3. Continue cooking until the mixture resembles thick strawberry preserves.
  4. Puree for a smooth ribbon, or leave a few tiny strawberry pieces for texture.
  5. Cool completely before using.

4. Make the Shortcake Crumble

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Combine the flour, sugar, and salt.
  3. Cut in the cold butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  4. Add the vanilla and heavy cream and gently mix until irregular clusters form.
  5. Spread on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  6. Bake for 12–16 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until golden brown and crisp.
  7. Cool completely. For additional buttery flavor, optionally toss with 1 tablespoon melted butter and a small pinch of flaky sea salt.

5. Churn the Ice Cream

  1. Pour the thoroughly chilled strawberry cheesecake base into your ice cream maker and churn according to the manufacturer's directions.
  2. During the final minute of churning, add about 1/2 cup of the shortcake crumble.

6. Add the Strawberry Ribbon

  1. Transfer about 1 inch of churned ice cream into a freezer-safe container.
  2. Spoon thin ribbons of strawberry sauce over the ice cream.
  3. Add another layer of ice cream followed by another thin layer of strawberry ribbon.
  4. Continue layering until the container is full, using about 1/4 cup strawberry ribbon total.
  5. For more pronounced swirls, make one or two gentle passes through the ice cream with a butter knife. Do not stir.
  6. Freeze for 4–6 hours or until firm.
