Brown Butter Banana Nut Bread Ice Cream

Yield: About 1 quart

Ingredients

Ice Cream Base

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup light brown sugar
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste or extract
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg

Roasted Bananas

  • 4 ripe bananas
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch salt

Banana Bread Streusel

  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/4 cup old-fashioned oats
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch salt
  • 4 tbsp cold butter
  • 1/2 cup chopped toasted pecans or walnuts (optional)

Swirl

  • About 1/4 cup salted caramel swirl

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Roast banana chunks with butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, vanilla, and salt for 20–25 minutes. Blend smooth.
  2. Heat oven to 350°F. Combine streusel ingredients except nuts, cut in butter, and bake 15–18 minutes. Cool and add toasted nuts if desired.
  3. Brown 4 tbsp butter until deep golden. Add cream, milk, and milk powder and heat until steaming.
  4. Whisk yolks with both sugars. Temper with hot dairy, return to pan, and cook to 170–175°F.
  5. Add banana puree, vanilla, salt, cinnamon, and nutmeg. Blend smooth and chill overnight.
  6. Churn. Add streusel and nuts during the final minute.
  7. Layer with thin ribbons of salted caramel, using about 1/4 cup total. Freeze 4–6 hours.
