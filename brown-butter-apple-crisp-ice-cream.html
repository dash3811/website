Brown Butter Apple Crisp Ice Cream

Roasted apple and brown-butter ice cream with cinnamon oat crisp and a restrained salted caramel apple ribbon.

Yield: About 1 quart

Ingredients

Roasted Cinnamon Apples

  • 3 medium Honeycrisp apples
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp light brown sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 3/4 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/8 tsp nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch kosher salt

Brown Butter Apple Base

  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp light corn syrup
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste or extract
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
  • About 1 1/2 cups roasted apple puree
  • 1-2 tbsp freeze-dried apple powder (optional, recommended)
  • 1 tbsp bourbon (optional)
  • 1/8 tsp almond extract (optional instead of bourbon)

Brown Butter Oat Crisp

  • 1/2 cup old-fashioned oats
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp light brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • Tiny pinch flaky sea salt (optional)

Salted Caramel Apple Ribbon

  • 1/2 cup reserved roasted apples
  • 2 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp dark brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt

Directions

1. Roast the Cinnamon Apples

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Peel and core the apples and cut them into roughly 1/2-inch cubes.
  3. Toss with melted butter, brown sugar, lemon juice, cinnamon, nutmeg, vanilla, and salt.
  4. Spread on a parchment-lined baking sheet and roast 25-35 minutes, stirring halfway through, until soft and caramelized with very little watery juice remaining.
  5. Cool completely. Reserve about 1/2 cup for the ribbon and puree the remaining apples until smooth.

2. Make the Brown Butter Apple Base

  1. Brown 4 tablespoons butter over medium heat until the milk solids are deep golden and nutty-smelling.
  2. Add heavy cream, whole milk, milk powder, and corn syrup. Whisk thoroughly and heat until steaming but not boiling.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk egg yolks with granulated sugar.
  4. Slowly whisk about 1 cup of the hot dairy into the yolks to temper them.
  5. Return everything to the saucepan and cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until it reaches 170-175°F.
  6. Remove from heat. Add roasted apple puree, vanilla, salt, cinnamon, optional freeze-dried apple powder, and either optional bourbon or almond extract.
  7. Blend until completely smooth.
  8. Cover and refrigerate at least 8 hours or overnight.

3. Make the Brown Butter Oat Crisp

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Brown the butter until golden brown and nutty-smelling.
  3. Combine oats, flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt.
  4. Pour in the browned butter and vanilla and mix until irregular clusters form.
  5. Spread on parchment and bake 12-16 minutes, stirring once halfway through.
  6. Cool completely. Optionally finish with a tiny pinch of flaky sea salt.

4. Make the Salted Caramel Apple Ribbon

  1. Combine reserved roasted apples, heavy cream, dark brown sugar, butter, vanilla, and salt in a small saucepan.
  2. Cook over medium-low heat for 5-7 minutes, crushing some of the apples while leaving tiny pieces intact.
  3. Continue until thick, sticky, and jammy.
  4. Cool completely before using.

5. Churn the Ice Cream

  1. Start the Cuisinart before pouring in the base. The paddle should already be moving when the liquid touches the frozen bowl. This helps prevent a thick rock-hard layer from freezing onto the bowl wall.
  2. Pour the thoroughly chilled base in steadily through the opening.
  3. Churn only until soft-serve consistency. Do not keep churning in an attempt to make it hard in the machine.
  4. During the final minute, add about 1/2 to 3/4 cup brown butter oat crisp.
  5. Transfer the ice cream out of the frozen bowl promptly when churning is finished.

6. Add the Apple-Caramel Ribbon

  1. Transfer about 1 inch of ice cream into a freezer-safe container.
  2. Spoon small streaks of apple-caramel ribbon over it.
  3. Continue layering ice cream and ribbon, using about 1/4 cup ribbon total.
  4. Make no more than one or two gentle passes with a butter knife if desired. Do not stir.
  5. Freeze 4-6 hours or until firm.

Tips & Upgrades

Apple Choice: Honeycrisp is a great all-around choice. For a more tart apple-pie flavor, use 2 Honeycrisp apples and 1 Granny Smith.
Freeze-Dried Apple: Grind freeze-dried apples into powder and add 1-2 tablespoons when blending in the roasted apple puree. This boosts apple flavor without adding extra water.
Keep Cinnamon in Check: The apple should be the first flavor you taste. Too much cinnamon will bury the roasted apple and brown-butter flavor.
Scoopability: The 1 1/2-cup cream / 1 1/2-cup milk balance and 2 tablespoons corn syrup are intentional. The corn syrup helps lower the freezing point so the finished ice cream stays softer in a normal home freezer.
Cuisinart Bowl Tip: Start the machine before pouring in the chilled base, pour steadily while the paddle is moving, and transfer the ice cream out as soon as it reaches soft-serve consistency. A very thin frozen film on the bowl wall is normal; a thick layer is what we're trying to avoid.
Bourbon Upgrade: Add 1 tablespoon bourbon to the finished custard before chilling for extra depth and slightly softer texture. If using bourbon, skip the almond extract on the first batch.
Salt Matters: A little salt in the base, crisp, and ribbon keeps the brown sugar and caramelized flavors from becoming cloying.

Brown Butter Apple Crisp Milkshake

Blend 3 generous scoops Brown Butter Apple Crisp Ice Cream with 1/4 cup whole milk, 1 tablespoon oat crisp, and 1 teaspoon apple-caramel ribbon until smooth and thick.

For more apple-crisp flavor throughout the shake, pulse 1 tablespoon oat crisp into crumbs in the blender first, then add the ice cream and milk. Top with whipped cream, more oat crisp, and a small spoonful of apple-caramel ribbon.
