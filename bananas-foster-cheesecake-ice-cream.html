Bananas Foster Cheesecake Ice Cream

Yield: About 1 quart

Caramelized bananas, brown butter, vanilla and rum cheesecake ice cream with graham cracker crumble and a restrained Bananas Foster ripple.

Ingredients

Bananas Foster

  • 4 ripe bananas
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp dark brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp dark rum (optional but recommended)

Cheesecake Base

  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp light corn syrup
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 3 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • About 1 1/2 cups prepared Bananas Foster

Graham Cracker Crumble

  • 1 cup coarsely crushed graham crackers
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter (brown it for best flavor)
  • 1 tbsp light brown sugar
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch kosher salt

Bananas Foster Ripple

  • Reserved 1/2 cup Bananas Foster
  • 1 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1 tsp dark brown sugar
  • Pinch kosher salt
  • 1 tsp dark rum (optional)

Directions

1. Make the Bananas Foster

  1. Slice bananas about 1/2 inch thick.
  2. Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add brown sugar and cook about 1 minute.
  3. Add bananas, cinnamon, and salt. Cook 3-5 minutes, gently turning until caramelized and very soft.
  4. Remove from heat and add vanilla and optional rum. No flambé is necessary.
  5. Cool completely. Reserve about 1/2 cup for the ripple and use about 1 1/2 cups for the base.

2. Make the Cheesecake Base

  1. Combine cream, milk, dry milk powder, and corn syrup. Whisk thoroughly and heat until steaming.
  2. Whisk egg yolks with sugar. Slowly whisk about 1 cup hot dairy into the yolks, then return everything to the saucepan.
  3. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, to 170-175°F.
  4. Remove from heat and add cream cheese, vanilla, salt, and about 1 1/2 cups Bananas Foster.
  5. Blend completely smooth and chill at least 8 hours, preferably overnight.

3. Make the Graham Cracker Crumble

  1. Heat oven to 325°F.
  2. For best flavor, brown the butter until golden and nutty.
  3. Combine graham crackers, butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt, leaving small clusters.
  4. Spread on parchment and bake 7-10 minutes. Cool completely.

4. Make the Bananas Foster Ripple

  1. Put the reserved Bananas Foster in a small saucepan with cream, brown sugar, salt, and optional rum.
  2. Cook over medium-low heat 3-5 minutes while crushing the banana.
  3. Reduce until very thick and jammy. A spoon dragged across the pan should leave a trail.
  4. Cool completely.

5. Churn

  1. Turn the Cuisinart on first so the paddle is moving before the base touches the frozen bowl.
  2. Pour in the thoroughly chilled base and churn to soft-serve consistency.
  3. During the final 30-60 seconds, add about 1/2 cup graham crumble.
  4. Transfer the ice cream out promptly.

6. Layer the Bananas Foster Ripple

  1. Layer about 1 inch of ice cream in a freezer container, followed by thin streaks of cold banana ripple and a little additional graham crumble.
  2. Repeat, using roughly 1/4 cup banana ripple and 1/4 cup additional crumble total.
  3. Do not stir.
  4. Freeze 4-6 hours.

Tips & Upgrades

Use very ripe bananas: Heavily spotted bananas give stronger banana aroma and natural sweetness without needing extra sugar.
Use dark rum if available: One tablespoon gives the classic Bananas Foster character and contributes slightly to scoopability. Flambéing is unnecessary.
Skip a separate caramel swirl: The concentrated caramelized-banana ripple already provides the brown-sugar and butter character without making the finished ice cream overly sweet.
Brown the crumble butter: Brown butter and banana work particularly well together. Keep some crumble airtight outside the ice cream for a crunchy topping.
Scoopability: The lighter cream-to-milk balance, 3 oz cream cheese, and corn syrup are intentional so the cheesecake base remains rich without becoming excessively dense.

Bananas Foster Cheesecake Milkshake

Blend 3 generous scoops ice cream with 1/4 cup whole milk, 1 tbsp Bananas Foster ripple, and 1 tbsp graham crumble. Pulse some graham crumble with the milk first for crust flavor throughout the shake. Top with whipped cream, fresh crumble, and a little banana ripple.
