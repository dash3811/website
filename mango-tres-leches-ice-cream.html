Mango Tres Leches Ice Cream

Yield: About 1 quart

Mango-vanilla ice cream with tres-leches cake crumble and a concentrated mango ribbon. No freeze-dried fruit required.

🛒 Open AnyList Import Page

Ingredients

Concentrated Mango

  • 3 cups ripe mango, diced
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • Pinch kosher salt

Mango Tres Leches Base

  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 1/4 cups whole milk
  • 1/4 cup evaporated milk
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp light corn syrup
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1 cup concentrated mango puree
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Tres Leches Cake Crumble

  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp evaporated milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tbsp sweetened condensed milk

Mango Ribbon

  • 1/2 cup concentrated mango puree
  • 1 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 tsp lime juice
  • Pinch kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla

Directions

1. Concentrate the Mango

  1. Combine mango, sugar, lime juice and salt in a saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Reduce to medium-low and cook 15-20 minutes, stirring frequently, until noticeably thicker.
  3. Blend completely smooth and cool. Reserve 1 cup for the base and 1/2 cup for the ribbon.

2. Make the Mango Tres Leches Base

  1. Combine cream, whole milk, evaporated milk, milk powder and corn syrup. Whisk and heat until steaming, not boiling.
  2. Whisk egg yolks with sugar. Slowly whisk about 1 cup hot dairy into the yolks, then return everything to the saucepan.
  3. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, to 170-175°F.
  4. Remove from heat; add vanilla and salt. Cool about 10 minutes, then blend in 1 cup concentrated mango puree.
  5. Chill at least 8 hours, preferably overnight.

3. Make the Tres Leches Cake Crumble

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Brown the butter until golden and nutty.
  2. Combine flour, milk powder, sugar, baking powder and salt.
  3. Add brown butter, evaporated milk, vanilla and condensed milk. Mix just until irregular cake-like clusters form.
  4. Spread on parchment and bake 12-16 minutes, stirring once halfway through. Cool completely.

4. Make the Mango Ribbon

  1. Combine reserved mango puree, condensed milk, lime juice, salt and vanilla.
  2. Cook over medium-low heat 3-5 minutes until thick and preserve-like. Cool completely.

5. Churn

  1. Turn the Cuisinart on first so the paddle is moving.
  2. Steadily pour in the thoroughly chilled base and churn to soft-serve consistency.
  3. During the last 30-60 seconds add about 1/2 cup cake crumble. Transfer out promptly.

6. Layer

  1. Add about 1 inch of ice cream to the freezer container, then thin streaks of mango ribbon and a little crumble.
  2. Repeat, using about 1/4 cup ribbon and 1/4 cup additional crumble total.
  3. Do not stir; one gentle butter-knife pass is enough if desired.
  4. Freeze 4-6 hours.

Tips & Upgrades

Mango ripeness: Use fragrant, ripe mangoes that give slightly when pressed. Frozen mango works too; thaw and drain first.
Keep sweetness controlled: The recipe uses evaporated milk in the base and only small amounts of condensed milk so the tres-leches character does not overwhelm the mango.
Optional coconut: Replace 1/4 cup whole milk with coconut cream for a mango-coconut variation.
Optional rum: Add 1 tbsp dark rum to the finished custard before chilling for another layer of flavor and a slight scoopability boost.
Save some crumble: Keep extra crumble airtight and add it fresh when serving for contrasting crunch.

Mango Tres Leches Milkshake

Blend 3 generous scoops ice cream with 1/4 cup whole milk, 1 tbsp cake crumble and 1 tbsp mango ribbon. Pulse the crumble with the milk first, then add the ice cream and ribbon. Top with whipped cream, mango ribbon and fresh crumble.
