Meal Plan — Week 1

Thursday, September 24 – Wednesday, September 30, 2026 · 1 adult · high protein · grocery budget $70

The ingredients below are the groceries to buy. Import this page as one recipe in AnyList, then choose “Add all ingredients to list.” The meat, celery, and green onions you already have are listed separately so they will not be added to your shopping list.

Ingredients: grocery list

Produce

  • 4 bananas
  • 3 apples
  • 2 bell peppers
  • 1 yellow onion
  • 2 pounds potatoes
  • 1 pound carrots

Dairy & eggs

  • 2 tubs plain Greek yogurt (32 ounces each)
  • 8 ounces shredded cheese
  • 1 dozen large eggs

Frozen

  • 2 bags frozen broccoli (12 ounces each)
  • 1 bag frozen mixed vegetables (12 to 16 ounces)

Pantry

  • 1 container old-fashioned oats (18 ounces)
  • 1 bag rice (2 pounds)
  • 1 package pasta (1 pound)
  • 1 loaf bread
  • 1 package tortillas (at least 8)
  • 2 cans black beans (15 ounces each)
  • 1 jar tomato pasta sauce (about 24 ounces)
  • 1 small jar peanut butter

Estimated checkout: about $40–$60 depending on local prices. This assumes cooking oil and basic seasonings are on hand.

Already on hand — do not buy

2 chicken breasts; 3 kielbasas; 2 sweet Italian pork sausage links; 10 ounces chicken cutlets; 10 ounces ground pork; 10 ounces boneless pork chops; 1 pound drumsticks; 2 thick boneless pork chops; celery; green onions.

Daily meals and recipes

Each day is planned for one person. The portions saved from one meal are used on the day named below.

Thursday · Friday · Saturday · Sunday · Monday · Tuesday · Wednesday

Thursday, September 24

Breakfast: Eggs and toast

For this meal: 2 eggs; 2 slices bread; 1 green onion

Make it: Scramble or fry the eggs, toast the bread, and finish with sliced green onion. About 8 minutes.

Lunch: Yogurt, oats, and banana

For this meal: 3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt; 1/3 cup oats; 1 banana

Make it: Mix yogurt and oats; slice the banana over the top. Assemble when ready to eat. About 3 minutes.

Dinner: Chicken cutlets, rice, and broccoli

For this meal: 10 oz chicken cutlets; 1/2 cup dry rice; about 1/2 bag broccoli (6 oz); oil, salt, and pepper

Make it: Cook the rice. Season and pan-cook the cutlets until the thickest part reaches 165°F. Steam broccoli. Eat half the chicken and roughly half the rice and broccoli; refrigerate the rest for Friday lunch. About 25 minutes.

Snack: Apple with peanut butter

For this meal: 1 apple; 1 tbsp peanut butter

Make it: Slice the apple and serve with peanut butter.

Friday, September 25

Breakfast: Overnight oats

For this meal: 1/2 cup oats; 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt; 1/2 banana; water to loosen

Make it: Stir oats, yogurt, and a splash of water together the night before; refrigerate. Top with sliced banana in the morning. Grab and go.

Lunch: Leftover chicken rice bowl

For this meal: Thursday's reserved chicken, rice, and broccoli

Make it: Reheat until hot. Add chopped green onion if you like. About 3 minutes.

Dinner: Sausage and pepper pasta

For this meal: 2 sweet Italian pork sausage links; 1 bell pepper; 1/2 onion; 4 oz dry pasta; about 1 cup tomato sauce

Make it: Boil pasta. Slice sausage and cook through with sliced pepper and onion; stir in sauce and pasta. Divide into two portions; refrigerate one for Saturday lunch. About 25 minutes.

Snack: Hard-boiled egg

For this meal: 1 egg

Make it: Boil ahead or use a prepared egg.

Saturday, September 26

Breakfast: Eggs and potatoes

For this meal: 2 eggs; 1 small potato; sliced green onion

Make it: Dice and microwave the potato until tender, then brown in a pan. Add eggs and green onion. About 10–15 minutes.

Lunch: Leftover sausage pasta

For this meal: Friday's reserved sausage, pepper, and pasta

Make it: Reheat until hot. About 3 minutes.

Dinner: Pork and black-bean rice skillet

For this meal: 10 oz ground pork; 1 can black beans, drained; 2 celery stalks; 1/2 onion; 1/2 cup dry rice; 2 green onions; oil and spices

Make it: Cook rice. Brown pork thoroughly with chopped celery and onion; stir in drained beans, spices, and green onions. Serve half over half the rice and refrigerate the other halves for Sunday dinner. About 25 minutes.

Snack: Greek yogurt

For this meal: 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt

Make it: Serve chilled.

Sunday, September 27

Breakfast: Yogurt oat banana bowl

For this meal: 3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt; 1/3 cup oats; 1 banana

Make it: Combine in a bowl. About 3 minutes.

Lunch: Kielbasa and pepper rice bowl

For this meal: 1 kielbasa; 1 bell pepper; 1/2 cup dry rice; sliced green onion

Make it: Cook rice. Slice kielbasa and pepper, brown in a skillet, and serve over rice with green onion. Save extra if the kielbasa is large. About 20 minutes.

Dinner: Leftover pork and bean stuffed potato

For this meal: Saturday's reserved pork, beans, and rice; 1 medium potato; 1 oz shredded cheese; green onion

Make it: Microwave potato until tender; split and top with heated pork and beans, cheese, and green onion. Serve reserved rice on the side if wanted. Cook both chicken breasts tonight: season and cook to 165°F, then refrigerate separately for Monday lunch and dinner. Potato: about 10 minutes; chicken prep: about 20 minutes.

Snack: Apple with peanut butter

For this meal: 1 apple; 1 tbsp peanut butter

Make it: Slice and serve.

Monday, September 28

Breakfast: Eggs and toast

For this meal: 2 eggs; 2 slices bread; green onion

Make it: Cook eggs, toast bread, and add sliced green onion. About 8 minutes.

Lunch: Chicken and broccoli rice bowl

For this meal: 1 chicken breast cooked Sunday; 1/2 cup cooked rice; about 1/4 bag broccoli (3 oz)

Make it: Steam broccoli, slice chicken, and reheat everything until hot. About 5 minutes.

Dinner: Leftover chicken fried rice

For this meal: 1 chicken breast cooked Sunday; 1 cup chilled cooked rice; 1 egg; about 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables; 1 green onion; oil and soy sauce if available

Make it: Heat rice and vegetables in a large skillet. Add diced chicken and cook until hot. Push to one side, scramble the egg, then mix together and finish with green onion. About 15 minutes.

Snack: Greek yogurt

For this meal: 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt

Make it: Serve chilled.

Tuesday, September 29

Breakfast: Overnight oats

For this meal: 1/2 cup oats; 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt; 1/2 banana; water to loosen

Make it: Stir oats and yogurt together the night before with a splash of water. Add banana in the morning.

Lunch: Black-bean cheese quesadilla

For this meal: 1 tortilla; 1/2 can black beans, drained; 1 oz shredded cheese; green onion

Make it: Fill tortilla, fold, and toast in a dry skillet until cheese melts. About 7 minutes. Refrigerate the remaining half-can of beans.

Dinner: Pork chops, potatoes, and broccoli

For this meal: 10 oz boneless pork chops; 1 medium potato; about 1/2 bag broccoli (6 oz); oil and seasonings

Make it: Dice and microwave potato until nearly tender. Pan-cook seasoned chops to 145°F, then rest 3 minutes; brown potato and steam broccoli. Eat half and refrigerate half for Wednesday lunch. About 25 minutes.

Snack: Banana and hard-boiled egg

For this meal: 1 banana; 1 egg

Make it: Grab and go.

Wednesday, September 30

Breakfast: Eggs and toast

For this meal: 2 eggs; 2 slices bread; green onion

Make it: Cook eggs, toast bread, and add green onion. About 8 minutes.

Lunch: Leftover pork chop plate

For this meal: Tuesday's reserved pork chop, potatoes, and broccoli

Make it: Reheat until hot. About 3 minutes.

Dinner: Roasted drumsticks and vegetables

For this meal: 1 lb chicken drumsticks; 1 medium potato; 2 carrots; 2 celery stalks; oil and seasonings

Make it: Heat oven to 425°F. Cut vegetables into bite-size pieces, toss with oil and seasonings, and spread beside drumsticks on a sheet pan. Roast about 35–45 minutes, until chicken reaches 165°F. Use the remaining black beans on the side if desired.

Snack: Greek yogurt

For this meal: 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt

Make it: Serve chilled.

Prep and storage

Refrigerate cooked leftovers promptly. Freeze raw meat planned for later in the week if it will not be cooked within its safe refrigerator window, then thaw it in the fridge. Freeze the two unused kielbasas and two thick boneless pork chops for another week.

Save Saturday's pork skillet for Sunday's stuffed potato and Sunday's chicken breast for Monday's fried rice. Cook rice ahead and chill it promptly for fried rice.
