Meal Plan — Week 1

Thursday, September 24 – Wednesday, September 30, 2026 · 1 adult · high protein · grocery budget $70

Daily meals and recipes

Each day is planned for one person. The portions saved from one meal are used on the day named below.

Thursday · Friday · Saturday · Sunday · Monday · Tuesday · Wednesday

Thursday, September 24

Breakfast: Eggs and toast

Ingredients
2 eggs; 2 slices bread; 1 green onion

Directions
Scramble or fry the eggs, toast the bread, and finish with sliced green onion. About 8 minutes.

Lunch: Yogurt, oats, and banana

Ingredients
3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt; 1/3 cup oats; 1 banana

Directions
Mix yogurt and oats; slice the banana over the top. Assemble when ready to eat. About 3 minutes.

Dinner: Chicken cutlets, rice, and broccoli

Ingredients
10 oz chicken cutlets; 1/2 cup dry rice; about 1/2 bag broccoli (6 oz); oil, salt, and pepper

Directions
Cook the rice. Season and pan-cook the cutlets until the thickest part reaches 165°F. Steam broccoli. Eat half the chicken and roughly half the rice and broccoli; refrigerate the rest for Friday lunch. About 25 minutes.

Snack: Apple with peanut butter

Ingredients
1 apple; 1 tbsp peanut butter

Directions
Slice the apple and serve with peanut butter.

Friday, September 25

Breakfast: Overnight oats

Ingredients
1/2 cup oats; 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt; 1/2 banana; water to loosen

Directions
Stir oats, yogurt, and a splash of water together the night before; refrigerate. Top with sliced banana in the morning. Grab and go.

Lunch: Leftover chicken rice bowl

Ingredients
Thursday's reserved chicken, rice, and broccoli

Directions
Reheat until hot. Add chopped green onion if you like. About 3 minutes.

Dinner: Sausage and pepper pasta

Ingredients
2 sweet Italian pork sausage links; 1 bell pepper; 1/2 onion; 4 oz dry pasta; about 1 cup tomato sauce

Directions
Boil pasta. Slice sausage and cook through with sliced pepper and onion; stir in sauce and pasta. Divide into two portions; refrigerate one for Saturday lunch. About 25 minutes.

Snack: Hard-boiled egg

Ingredients
1 egg

Directions
Boil ahead or use a prepared egg.

Saturday, September 26

Breakfast: Eggs and potatoes

Ingredients
2 eggs; 1 small potato; sliced green onion

Directions
Dice and microwave the potato until tender, then brown in a pan. Add eggs and green onion. About 10–15 minutes.

Lunch: Leftover sausage pasta

Ingredients
Friday's reserved sausage, pepper, and pasta

Directions
Reheat until hot. About 3 minutes.

Dinner: Pork and black-bean rice skillet

Ingredients
10 oz ground pork; 1 can black beans, drained; 2 celery stalks; 1/2 onion; 1/2 cup dry rice; 2 green onions; oil and spices

Directions
Cook rice. Brown pork thoroughly with chopped celery and onion; stir in drained beans, spices, and green onions. Serve half over half the rice and refrigerate the other halves for Sunday dinner. About 25 minutes.

Snack: Greek yogurt

Ingredients
1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt

Directions
Serve chilled.

Sunday, September 27

Breakfast: Yogurt oat banana bowl

Ingredients
3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt; 1/3 cup oats; 1 banana

Directions
Combine in a bowl. About 3 minutes.

Lunch: Kielbasa and pepper rice bowl

Ingredients
1 kielbasa; 1 bell pepper; 1/2 cup dry rice; sliced green onion

Directions
Cook rice. Slice kielbasa and pepper, brown in a skillet, and serve over rice with green onion. Save extra if the kielbasa is large. About 20 minutes.

Dinner: Leftover pork and bean stuffed potato

Ingredients
Saturday's reserved pork, beans, and rice; 1 medium potato; 1 oz shredded cheese; green onion

Directions
Microwave potato until tender; split and top with heated pork and beans, cheese, and green onion. Serve reserved rice on the side if wanted. Cook both chicken breasts tonight: season and cook to 165°F, then refrigerate separately for Monday lunch and dinner. Potato: about 10 minutes; chicken prep: about 20 minutes.

Snack: Apple with peanut butter

Ingredients
1 apple; 1 tbsp peanut butter

Directions
Slice and serve.

Monday, September 28

Breakfast: Eggs and toast

Ingredients
2 eggs; 2 slices bread; green onion

Directions
Cook eggs, toast bread, and add sliced green onion. About 8 minutes.

Lunch: Chicken and broccoli rice bowl

Ingredients
1 chicken breast cooked Sunday; 1/2 cup cooked rice; about 1/4 bag broccoli (3 oz)

Directions
Steam broccoli, slice chicken, and reheat everything until hot. About 5 minutes.

Dinner: Leftover chicken fried rice

Ingredients
1 chicken breast cooked Sunday; 1 cup chilled cooked rice; 1 egg; about 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables; 1 green onion; oil and soy sauce if available

Directions
Heat rice and vegetables in a large skillet. Add diced chicken and cook until hot. Push to one side, scramble the egg, then mix together and finish with green onion. About 15 minutes.

Snack: Greek yogurt

Ingredients
1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt

Directions
Serve chilled.

Tuesday, September 29

Breakfast: Overnight oats

Ingredients
1/2 cup oats; 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt; 1/2 banana; water to loosen

Directions
Stir oats and yogurt together the night before with a splash of water. Add banana in the morning.

Lunch: Black-bean cheese quesadilla

Ingredients
1 tortilla; 1/2 can black beans, drained; 1 oz shredded cheese; green onion

Directions
Fill tortilla, fold, and toast in a dry skillet until cheese melts. About 7 minutes. Refrigerate the remaining half-can of beans.

Dinner: Pork chops, potatoes, and broccoli

Ingredients
10 oz boneless pork chops; 1 medium potato; about 1/2 bag broccoli (6 oz); oil and seasonings

Directions
Dice and microwave potato until nearly tender. Pan-cook seasoned chops to 145°F, then rest 3 minutes; brown potato and steam broccoli. Eat half and refrigerate half for Wednesday lunch. About 25 minutes.

Snack: Banana and hard-boiled egg

Ingredients
1 banana; 1 egg

Directions
Grab and go.

Wednesday, September 30

Breakfast: Eggs and toast

Ingredients
2 eggs; 2 slices bread; green onion

Directions
Cook eggs, toast bread, and add green onion. About 8 minutes.

Lunch: Leftover pork chop plate

Ingredients
Tuesday's reserved pork chop, potatoes, and broccoli

Directions
Reheat until hot. About 3 minutes.

Dinner: Roasted drumsticks and vegetables

Ingredients
1 lb chicken drumsticks; 1 medium potato; 2 carrots; 2 celery stalks; oil and seasonings

Directions
Heat oven to 425°F. Cut vegetables into bite-size pieces, toss with oil and seasonings, and spread beside drumsticks on a sheet pan. Roast about 35–45 minutes, until chicken reaches 165°F. Use the remaining black beans on the side if desired.

Snack: Greek yogurt

Ingredients
1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt

Directions
Serve chilled.

Dairy & eggs

Frozen

  • 1 bag frozen mixed vegetables (12 to 16 ounces)

Pantry

  • 1 container old-fashioned oats (18 ounces)
  • 1 bag rice (2 pounds)
  • 1 package pasta (1 pound)
  • 1 loaf bread
  • 1 package tortillas (at least 8)
  • 2 cans black beans (15 ounces each)
  • 1 jar tomato pasta sauce (about 24 ounces)
  • 1 small jar peanut butter

Estimated checkout: about $40–$60 depending on local prices. This assumes cooking oil and basic seasonings are on hand.

Already on hand — do not buy

2 chicken breasts; 3 kielbasas; 2 sweet Italian pork sausage links; 10 ounces chicken cutlets; 10 ounces ground pork; 10 ounces boneless pork chops; 1 pound drumsticks; 2 thick boneless pork chops; celery; green onions.

Prep and storage

Refrigerate cooked leftovers promptly. Freeze raw meat planned for later in the week if it will not be cooked within its safe refrigerator window, then thaw it in the fridge. Freeze the two unused kielbasas and two thick boneless pork chops for another week.

Save Saturday's pork skillet for Sunday's stuffed potato and Sunday's chicken breast for Monday's fried rice. Cook rice ahead and chill it promptly for fried rice.
