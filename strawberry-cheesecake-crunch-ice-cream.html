Strawberry Cheesecake Crunch Ice Cream

Yield: About 1 quart

Creamy vanilla cheesecake ice cream with concentrated strawberry ripple and strawberry-shortcake-bar-style cookie crunch. No freeze-dried fruit required.

Ingredients

Strawberry Ripple

  • 1 lb fresh strawberries, hulled and quartered
  • 3 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch kosher salt

Cheesecake Base

  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp light corn syrup
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 3 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Strawberry Cheesecake Crunch

  • 12 Golden Oreos
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp strawberry gelatin powder
  • Pinch kosher salt

Directions

1. Make the Strawberry Ripple

  1. Combine strawberries, sugar, lemon juice, vanilla, and salt in a saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Once the berries release juice, reduce to medium-low and simmer 15-25 minutes, stirring frequently and crushing the berries.
  3. Reduce until it resembles loose strawberry preserves rather than sauce. Blend smooth if desired, then refrigerate until completely cold.

2. Make the Cheesecake Base

  1. Combine cream, milk, dry milk powder, and corn syrup. Whisk thoroughly and heat until steaming but not boiling.
  2. Whisk egg yolks with sugar. Slowly whisk about 1 cup hot dairy into the yolks, then return everything to the saucepan.
  3. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, to 170-175°F.
  4. Remove from heat. Add cream cheese, vanilla, and salt and blend completely smooth.
  5. Chill at least 8 hours, preferably overnight.

3. Make the Strawberry Cheesecake Crunch

  1. Heat oven to 325°F.
  2. Coarsely crush the Golden Oreos, leaving different-sized pieces.
  3. Divide crumbs approximately in half and mix strawberry gelatin powder into one half.
  4. Mix melted butter into both portions, recombine, spread on parchment, and bake 7-10 minutes.
  5. Cool completely.

4. Churn

  1. Turn the Cuisinart on before adding anything so the paddle is moving.
  2. Pour in the thoroughly chilled base and churn to soft-serve consistency.
  3. During the last 30-60 seconds, add about 1/2 cup crunch. Transfer out promptly.

5. Layer the Strawberry Ripple

  1. Layer about 1 inch of ice cream in a freezer container, then add thin streaks of cold strawberry ripple and a little crunch.
  2. Repeat, using about 1/4-1/3 cup ripple and about 1/4 cup additional crunch total.
  3. Do not stir. Make at most one gentle pass with a butter knife.
  4. Freeze 4-6 hours.

Tips & Upgrades

Keep some crunch outside the ice cream: Store extra crunch airtight and sprinkle it over each serving. The inside pieces soften slightly while the fresh topping stays genuinely crunchy.
Scoopability: The 3 oz cream cheese and 2 tbsp corn syrup are intentional to preserve cheesecake flavor without repeating the overly dense texture of the earlier strawberry batch.
Strawberry concentration: Reduce the berries until preserve-like. Removing excess water intensifies strawberry flavor and helps prevent iciness without freeze-dried fruit.

Strawberry Cheesecake Crunch Milkshake

Blend 3 generous scoops ice cream, 1/4 cup whole milk, 1 tbsp strawberry ripple, and 2 tbsp strawberry crunch. Pulse 1 tbsp of the crunch with the milk first, then add the remaining ingredients. Top with whipped cream, strawberry ripple, and fresh crunch.
