Pineapple Upside-Down Cake Ice Cream

Roasted pineapple ice cream with caramelized pineapple ribbon, buttery brown-sugar cake pieces, and little flashes of cherry.

Yield: About 1 quart

Ingredients

Roasted Pineapple

  • 2 cups fresh pineapple, cut into small chunks
  • 1 tbsp light brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch kosher salt

Scoopable Pineapple-Vanilla Base

  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp light corn syrup
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste or extract
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 1/2 cups roasted pineapple, pureed
  • 1-2 tbsp freeze-dried pineapple powder (optional, highly recommended)
  • 1 tbsp dark rum (optional)

Caramelized Pineapple Ribbon

  • 1/2 cup reserved roasted pineapple
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp dark brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp pineapple juice
  • Pinch salt
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla

Brown Butter Cake Crumble

  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp light brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tbsp heavy cream

Cherry & Optional Mix-In

  • 4-6 maraschino cherries, drained extremely well and finely diced
  • 1/3 cup finely chopped toasted pecans (optional)

Directions

1. Roast the Pineapple

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Toss pineapple with brown sugar, melted butter, vanilla, and salt. Spread on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  3. Roast 25-35 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the edges are browned and very little watery juice remains.
  4. Cool completely. Reserve 1/2 cup for the ribbon and puree the remaining 1 1/2 cups until smooth.

2. Make the Scoopable Pineapple-Vanilla Base

  1. Combine heavy cream, whole milk, milk powder, and corn syrup in a saucepan. Whisk well and heat until steaming but not boiling.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk egg yolks with granulated sugar.
  3. Slowly whisk about 1 cup of the hot dairy into the yolks to temper them.
  4. Return everything to the saucepan and cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until it reaches 170-175°F.
  5. Remove from heat. Add vanilla, salt, pineapple puree, optional freeze-dried pineapple powder, and optional dark rum.
  6. Blend until completely smooth.
  7. Cover and refrigerate at least 8 hours or overnight.

3. Make the Caramelized Pineapple Ribbon

  1. Combine reserved roasted pineapple, butter, dark brown sugar, pineapple juice, salt, and vanilla in a small saucepan.
  2. Cook over medium-low heat for 5-8 minutes, crushing the pineapple as it cooks.
  3. Continue until thick and jammy rather than syrupy.
  4. Cool completely before using.

4. Make the Brown Butter Cake Crumble

  1. Brown the butter over medium heat until golden brown and nutty-smelling. Let cool for several minutes.
  2. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  3. Combine flour, brown sugar, granulated sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  4. Add the brown butter, vanilla, and heavy cream. Mix only until irregular clusters form.
  5. Spread on a parchment-lined baking sheet and bake 12-16 minutes, stirring once halfway through.
  6. Cool completely.

5. Prepare the Cherries

  1. Drain 4-6 maraschino cherries extremely well and pat them dry with paper towels.
  2. Dice very finely so the finished ice cream gets small flashes of cherry rather than large frozen pieces.

6. Churn the Ice Cream

  1. Churn the thoroughly chilled pineapple base according to your machine's instructions.
  2. Stop at approximately soft-serve consistency. Do not keep churning in an attempt to make it hard in the machine.
  3. During the final minute, add about 1/2 cup brown-butter cake crumble, the finely diced cherries, and optional toasted pecans.

7. Add the Pineapple Ribbon

  1. Transfer about 1 inch of ice cream into a freezer-safe container.
  2. Spoon thin streaks of caramelized pineapple over it.
  3. Continue layering ice cream and pineapple ribbon, using about 1/4 cup ribbon total.
  4. Make only one or two gentle passes with a butter knife if you want more pronounced swirls. Do not stir.
  5. Freeze 4-6 hours or until firm.

Tips & Upgrades

Freeze-Dried Pineapple: Grind freeze-dried pineapple into powder and add 1-2 tablespoons when blending the roasted pineapple into the custard. This strengthens the pineapple flavor without adding more water. Highly recommended if available.
Scoopability: The 1 1/2-cup cream / 1 1/2-cup milk balance and 2 tablespoons corn syrup are intentional. The corn syrup helps suppress the freezing point so the ice cream stays softer in a normal home freezer. Stop churning at soft-serve consistency rather than over-churning.
Dark Rum: Add 1 tablespoon to the finished custard before chilling. Pineapple, brown sugar, butter, and rum work extremely well together, and the small amount of alcohol also contributes slightly to a softer texture.
Cherry Choice: Regular maraschino cherries work, but darker Luxardo-style cocktail cherries give a richer, less candy-like cherry flavor. Whichever you use, drain and dry them thoroughly.
Optional Pecans: Add about 1/3 cup finely chopped toasted pecans during the final minute of churning. For the first batch, leaving them out lets the pineapple and cake flavors take center stage.
Keep the Ribbon Distinct: Make sure the pineapple ribbon is completely cool and thick like jam before layering it. Use about 1/4 cup total and avoid stirring it into the ice cream.

Pineapple Upside-Down Cake Milkshake

Blend 3 generous scoops Pineapple Upside-Down Cake Ice Cream with 1/4 cup whole milk, 1 tablespoon brown-butter cake crumble, and 1 teaspoon pineapple ribbon until smooth and thick.

Top with whipped cream, a little cake crumble, and one cherry. Skip malted milk powder for this flavor so the roasted pineapple, browned butter, vanilla, and brown sugar stay prominent.
