Cinnamon Roll with Cream Cheese Frosting Ice Cream

2-quart Cuisinart formulation · Target finished yield: 1¾–2 quarts, including mix-ins. Chill 8 hours; harden 4–6 hours.

Cinnamon-vanilla custard, a rich brown-sugar cinnamon swirl, soft cream-cheese frosting ribbons and tender cinnamon-roll pieces.

Ingredients

Cinnamon-vanilla custard base

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 3/4 cups whole milk
  • 5 large egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup light corn syrup
  • 1/4 cup nonfat dry milk powder
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Brown-sugar cinnamon swirl

  • 1/3 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp light corn syrup
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt

Cream cheese frosting ribbon

  • 4 oz full-fat cream cheese, softened
  • 3 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 3 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Cinnamon roll pieces

  • 2 medium baked cinnamon rolls, preferably without icing
  • 1 tbsp melted butter
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon

Directions

1. Prepare the cinnamon-vanilla custard

  1. Combine cream, milk, dry milk powder and cinnamon in a saucepan. Whisk smooth and heat until steaming without boiling.
  2. Whisk egg yolks and granulated sugar in a separate bowl. Slowly whisk in about 1 cup hot dairy to temper.
  3. Return mixture to saucepan, add corn syrup and cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until 170–175°F.
  4. Remove from heat, add vanilla and salt, and blend briefly to distribute cinnamon.
  5. Cool rapidly in an ice bath; refrigerate at least 8 hours, preferably overnight. Taste cold to confirm a clear cinnamon-vanilla flavor.

2. Make the brown-sugar cinnamon swirl

  1. Melt butter in a small saucepan over medium-low heat.
  2. Add brown sugar, cream, cinnamon, corn syrup and salt. Stir smooth and simmer gently for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Cool completely; it should be thick and spoonable when cold. If too stiff, stir in 1 tsp cream.

3. Make the cream cheese frosting ribbon

  1. Beat softened cream cheese smooth.
  2. Beat in powdered sugar, vanilla and salt.
  3. Add cream 1 tbsp at a time until thick but spreadable. Refrigerate until assembly.

4. Prepare the cinnamon roll pieces

  1. Heat oven to 300°F. Cut baked, uniced cinnamon rolls into 1/4–1/2-inch pieces.
  2. Toss gently with melted butter and cinnamon.
  3. Bake on parchment 8–12 minutes until lightly toasted outside but still tender inside. Cool completely.

5. Churn the ice cream

  1. Freeze the Cuisinart bowl completely and stir the chilled custard.
  2. Start the machine before pouring in the custard. Stay below the manufacturer's maximum liquid fill line; churn in batches if necessary.
  3. Churn until soft-serve consistency, typically 20–30 minutes.
  4. Add about half the cinnamon roll pieces in the last 30–60 seconds. Transfer promptly to a chilled freezer container.

6. Layer and harden

  1. Spread one-third of ice cream in the freezer container.
  2. Drizzle one-third of cinnamon swirl, dot with one-third of frosting ribbon, and scatter some remaining cinnamon roll pieces.
  3. Repeat for two more layers. Make only one or two gentle knife passes so ribbons stay distinct.
  4. Cover tightly and freeze 4–6 hours.

Tips & upgrades

Flavor: Taste the custard cold before churning. It should clearly taste of cinnamon and vanilla; the swirl intensifies the cinnamon-roll character.
Texture: Keep cinnamon-roll pieces tender, not fully crunchy. Cream cheese frosting may freeze firm, so use thin ribbons rather than large pockets.
Capacity: A 2-quart machine's capacity does not guarantee it can hold 2 quarts of liquid base. Follow the maximum fill line and churn in batches if needed.

Cinnamon Roll Milkshake

For one large milkshake, blend 3 generous scoops ice cream, 1/4 cup cold whole milk, 1 tbsp cream cheese frosting and 1/2 tsp cinnamon swirl just until smooth. Top with whipped cream, a small cinnamon roll piece and a light drizzle of cinnamon filling.
