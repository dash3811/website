Toasted Coconut Cream Pie Ice Cream

Yield: About 1 quart

Deep toasted coconut, vanilla cream, buttery pie crust and a lightly salted coconut-caramel ripple.

Ingredients

Toasted Coconut

  • 1 1/2 cups sweetened shredded coconut

Toasted Coconut Base

  • 1 1/4 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 3/4 cup unsweetened coconut cream
  • 1 cup toasted coconut (from above)
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp light corn syrup
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp coconut extract (optional)

Brown-Butter Pie Crust Crumble

  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 5 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1-2 tbsp heavy cream

Salted Coconut-Caramel Ripple

  • 2 tbsp dark brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp coconut cream
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt

Directions

1. Toast the Coconut

  1. Heat oven to 325°F.
  2. Spread coconut evenly on a baking sheet and bake 6-10 minutes, stirring every 2-3 minutes, until deep golden brown.
  3. Reserve 1 cup for the base infusion and 1/2 cup for mix-in and topping.

2. Make the Toasted Coconut Base

  1. Combine heavy cream, milk, coconut cream and 1 cup toasted coconut. Heat until steaming but do not boil.
  2. Turn off heat, cover and steep 30 minutes.
  3. Strain through a fine-mesh strainer, pressing firmly on the coconut to recover the infused dairy.
  4. Return dairy to the saucepan. Whisk in milk powder and corn syrup and heat until steaming.
  5. Whisk egg yolks with sugar. Slowly whisk about 1 cup hot dairy into the yolks, then return everything to the saucepan.
  6. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, to 170-175°F.
  7. Remove from heat; add vanilla and salt. Blend briefly and chill at least 8 hours, preferably overnight.
  8. Taste the base ice cold before churning. It should taste strongly of coconut. If needed, stir in 2-3 tbsp additional coconut cream or 1/4 tsp coconut extract.

3. Make the Brown-Butter Pie Crust Crumble

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Brown the butter until deep golden and nutty.
  2. Combine flour, sugar and salt. Add brown butter and vanilla.
  3. Add 1 tbsp heavy cream and mix. Add the second tablespoon only if needed to form irregular pie-crust-like clusters.
  4. Spread on parchment and bake 12-16 minutes, stirring once. Cool completely and finish with a tiny pinch of salt.

4. Make the Salted Coconut-Caramel Ripple

  1. Combine brown sugar, coconut cream, butter and heavy cream in a small saucepan.
  2. Cook over medium-low heat 3-5 minutes, stirring frequently.
  3. Remove from heat and add vanilla and salt. Cool completely.
  4. If very stiff when cold, stir in 1 tsp coconut cream.

5. Churn

  1. Turn the Cuisinart on before adding the thoroughly chilled base so the paddle is already moving.
  2. Pour the base steadily through the opening and churn to soft-serve consistency.
  3. During the last 30-60 seconds add 1/3 cup reserved toasted coconut and 1/2 cup pie-crust crumble.
  4. Transfer out promptly.

6. Build the Coconut Cream Pie

  1. Add about 1 inch of ice cream to the freezer container, then a few thin streaks of coconut caramel and a little pie-crust crumble.
  2. Repeat, using about 1/4 cup caramel ripple and 1/4 cup additional crumble total.
  3. Do not stir. One gentle butter-knife pass is enough if desired.
  4. Freeze 4-6 hours.

Tips & Upgrades

Cold-base test: Do not churn if the cold base tastes merely creamy with a hint of coconut. The coconut should be obvious before freezing.
Coconut extract: If needed, use only 1/4 tsp to boost aroma without making the flavor artificial.
Save toppings: Keep some toasted coconut and pie crumble airtight outside the ice cream so each serving can have fresh crunch.
Salt matters: Coconut, brown sugar, cream and butter all benefit from enough salt to prevent the finished flavor from becoming cloying.

Toasted Coconut Cream Pie Milkshake

Blend 3 generous scoops ice cream with 1/4 cup whole milk, 1 tbsp pie-crust crumble, 1 tbsp toasted coconut and 1 tsp coconut-caramel ripple. Pulse the pie crust and coconut with the milk first, then add the ice cream and caramel. Top with whipped cream, toasted coconut, pie-crust crumble and a tiny drizzle of coconut caramel.
