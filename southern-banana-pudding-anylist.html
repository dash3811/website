Southern Banana Pudding Ice Cream

Ingredients for the 2-quart recipe. Import this page into AnyList using your normal recipe import workflow.

Ingredients

Banana custard base

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 3/4 cups whole milk
  • 3 medium very ripe bananas (about 1–1 1/4 cups mashed)
  • 5 large egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup light corn syrup
  • 1/4 cup nonfat dry milk powder
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp banana extract (optional, only if needed)

Concentrated banana ribbon

  • 2 medium very ripe bananas
  • 2 tbsp light brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp whole milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of kosher salt

Buttery Nilla Wafer crumble

  • 30 Nilla Wafers
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • Pinch of kosher salt
