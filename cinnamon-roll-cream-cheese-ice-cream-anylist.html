Cinnamon Roll with Cream Cheese Frosting Ice Cream

Ingredient-only page for importing into AnyList using your normal recipe import workflow.

Ingredients

Cinnamon-vanilla custard base

  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 3 tbsp light corn syrup
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3/8 tsp kosher salt

Brown-sugar cinnamon swirl

  • 1/4 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1 1/2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 1/2 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp light corn syrup
  • Pinch of kosher salt

Cream cheese frosting ribbon

  • 3 oz full-fat cream cheese, softened
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 2 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1/1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Cinnamon roll pieces

  • 1 medium baked cinnamon roll, preferably without icing
  • 2 tsp melted butter
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
