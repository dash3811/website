Cinnamon Roll with Cream Cheese Frosting Ice Cream

Ingredient-only page for importing into AnyList using your normal recipe import workflow.

Ingredients

Cinnamon-vanilla custard base

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 3/4 cups whole milk
  • 5 large egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup light corn syrup
  • 1/4 cup nonfat dry milk powder
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Brown-sugar cinnamon swirl

  • 1/3 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp light corn syrup
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt

Cream cheese frosting ribbon

  • 4 oz full-fat cream cheese, softened
  • 3 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 3 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Cinnamon roll pieces

  • 2 medium baked cinnamon rolls, preferably without icing
  • 1 tbsp melted butter
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
