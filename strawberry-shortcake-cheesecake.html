Strawberry Shortcake Cheesecake Ice Cream

Yield: About 1 quart

Ingredients

Roasted Strawberries

  • 1 lb fresh strawberries, hulled and quartered
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Ice Cream Base

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 3 tbsp nonfat dry milk powder
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste or extract
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 2/3 of roasted strawberries, pureed
  • 2 tbsp freeze-dried strawberry powder (optional)

Strawberry Ribbon

  • 1/3 of roasted strawberries
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Pinch of salt

Shortcake Crumble

  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 4 tbsp cold unsalted butter
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp melted butter (optional)
  • Pinch flaky sea salt (optional)

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Toss strawberries with sugar, lemon juice, vanilla, and salt. Roast 25–35 minutes, stirring once, until soft and syrupy. Cool. Reserve 1/3 and puree remaining 2/3.
  2. Combine cream, milk, and milk powder. Heat until steaming.
  3. Whisk yolks and 1/3 cup sugar. Temper with hot dairy, return to pan, and cook to 170–175°F.
  4. Remove from heat. Add cream cheese, vanilla, salt, strawberry puree, and optional strawberry powder. Blend smooth and refrigerate overnight.
  5. Cook reserved strawberries with ribbon sugar, lemon juice, and salt for 5–8 minutes until thick. Cool completely.
  6. For crumble, heat oven to 350°F. Combine flour, sugar, and salt. Cut in butter; add vanilla and cream. Bake clusters 12–16 minutes until golden. Cool.
  7. Churn base. Add about 1/2 cup crumble during final minute.
  8. Layer ice cream with about 1/4 cup strawberry ribbon. Freeze 4–6 hours.
