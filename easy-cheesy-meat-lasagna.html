Easy Cheesy Meat Lasagna

A fast, comforting lasagna with a rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta filling, and plenty of melted cheese—without boiling noodles first.

Yield: 6-8 servings   Prep: 15 minutes   Bake: about 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef or Italian sausage
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara or pasta sauce
  • 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce
  • 9 oven-ready lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)

Directions

1. Make the Meat Sauce

  1. Brown the ground beef or Italian sausage in a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Drain excess grease.
  3. Add the marinara, tomato sauce, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, black pepper, and optional red pepper flakes.
  4. Simmer for about 5 minutes.

2. Make the Cheese Filling

  1. Mix the ricotta, egg, 1/2 cup mozzarella, 1/4 cup Parmesan, and a pinch of salt and pepper until combined.

3. Assemble the Lasagna

  1. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Add 3 oven-ready noodles.
  3. Spread half of the ricotta mixture over the noodles.
  4. Add about one-third of the meat sauce and about 1/2 cup mozzarella.
  5. Repeat with another 3 noodles, the remaining ricotta mixture, another third of the sauce, and another 1/2 cup mozzarella.
  6. Top with the final 3 noodles, remaining meat sauce, remaining mozzarella, and remaining Parmesan.

4. Bake

  1. Cover the dish tightly with foil.
  2. Bake at 375°F for 35 minutes.
  3. Remove the foil and bake another 12-15 minutes, until the cheese is bubbling and lightly browned.
  4. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before slicing so the layers hold together.

Tips & Easy Upgrades

Best easy flavor upgrade: Use Italian sausage instead of plain ground beef, or use half beef and half sausage.
Extra cheesy: Add a few slices of provolone to the middle layer.
Fresh flavor: Mix 1-2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil into the ricotta if you have it.
Do not skip the rest: Give the finished lasagna 10 minutes before cutting so it slices cleanly instead of sliding apart.
